01 / Our Vision

Engineering concepts shouldn't stay on paper.

Chemical engineering becomes more intuitive when you can follow material through a plant, see what each unit does, and reason about changes to process conditions. NIRA Chemicals aims to bring that hands-on way of learning into an approachable digital workspace.

We're starting with education and training-oriented experiences, with a long-term vision for engineering analysis workflows built on validated models and transparent assumptions.

03 / The Experience

From a process idea to engineering insight.

A simple, planned workflow for exploring chemical processes without hiding the calculations behind the interface.

STEP 01
Choose or build

Start with a teaching example or build a process using common equipment blocks.

STEP 02
Change conditions

STEP 03
Understand results

Inspect calculations, model limitations, and AI-assisted engineering explanations.

04 / Where We Are

Early-stage · actively building

Built with curiosity. Guided by engineering rigor.

NIRA Chemicals is a student-led prototype. The focus is on usability, useful chemical engineering models, clear documentation, and separating educational demonstrations from validated simulation results.

The website illustrates our direction; it does not claim a production-ready simulator or industrial certification.

AI + Engineering

An AI copilot that helps you understand the process.

We envision integrating language models such as Claude to support equipment explanations, calculation walkthroughs, troubleshooting suggestions, and technical learning. AI suggestions would complement—not replace—verified engineering methods, safety reviews, and human judgment.
